# Recovered data audit

Audit date: 2026-11-08

This audit is read-only and uses deterministic samples of up to 256 windows from each array. The complete machine-readable report is generated by `scripts/audit_training_data.py`.

## Preliminary findings

| Source | Recovered shape | Median channel standard deviation | Main issue |
| --- | --- | ---: | --- |
| Alpha train | 8,214 × 14 × 256 | 0.75 | Scale differs across splits and sources |
| Attention train | 13,743 × 14 × 256 | 7.77 | Not z-scored |
| Crowdsource | 12,296 × 14 × 256 | 7.69 | Not z-scored |
| DREAMER | 170,246 × 14 × 256 | 3.64 | 27.5% of sampled channel-windows are constant |
| DriverDistraction | 116,140 × 14 × 256 | 12.31 | Count differs from the earlier 66,197-window record |
| STEW | 28,512 × 14 × 256 | 16.01 | Not z-scored |
| TUAB train | 1,497,600 × 16 × 256 | 0.45 | No recording IDs or sampling metadata |

All sampled arrays contained finite values. However, the Emotiv sources clearly do not share one normalization pipeline.

The recovered TUAB directory is named `TUAB_16ch_10s`, but each stored window contains 256 samples. At an assumed 128 Hz, this is two seconds. The files contain no sampling-rate metadata and no recording or subject identifiers. Five adjacent rows therefore must not be concatenated and labeled as a 10-second recording.

## Current decision

- Do not claim strict reproduction of the lost SAMBA-E or SAMBA-T checkpoints.
- Do not join the recovered arrays in one training loader without a new, explicit normalization policy.
- Do not infer duration from directory names.
- Do not concatenate TUAB rows without verified recording boundaries.
- Resolve DREAMER constant channels and the DriverDistraction count mismatch before training.

The single new `SAMBA` checkpoint will be released only after these checks are encoded in a versioned manifest and preprocessing pipeline.
